# Practical prototype testing plan (v4 low-flow turbine)

Nothing in this package has been measured. The gates below convert the model's assumptions into numbers, in the order that
unblocks the next decision. Every number here is read from the model's result files by `docs/make_test_plan_v4.py`.
Instruments: optical tachometer (through the guard ring), DC V/I logger (10 Hz), bench supply and load bank, a spring scale + thread
for torque, a vacuum pump with gauge, a float / probe for water speed, a thermometer, a dial indicator.

| # | Gate | Measurement | Acceptance / what it updates | On failure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | GL60 characterisation (bench, BEFORE machining) | KV (3 speeds, 3 line pairs, scope on the DC after a bridge: V_dc,oc / rpm is the number the model uses), R_LL, L_LL, cogging torque over a full turn (thread + scale on a 50 mm arm), no-load loss at 200 / 400 / 700 rpm (drive with a drill, measure the torque), hole patterns (stator / bell; which pattern is the stator's), lead exit, bell runout, hollow-bore diameter | KV 20.0-26.2 (model 21.5); R 4.5-6.5 ohm; cogging <= 30 mN m for a self-start at 0.6 m/s (17 for 0.5, 45 for 0.7; model assumes 24); no-load <= 0.21 W at 300 rpm | update `generator_lowflow.GEN`, re-run `optimize_blades.py 1 2`, `feasibility.py`, `coupled_eval.py`; cogging above 45 mN m means hand-start below 0.8 m/s |
| G2 | Breakaway and running friction of the sealed stub shaft | front cap + stub + seal + bearing + DIN 472 ring on the bench, wet: breakaway and running torque at 100 / 300 / 600 rpm | breakaway <= 18 mN m (model 12 without cogging); running <= 10 mN m (model 7) | NBR seal excluded; polish the journal (or fit the hardened sleeve); check seal squeeze; seal x2 costs 23 % at 0.5 m/s |
| G3 | Printed coupons | the REAL rear hub half (pockets print as bridged tunnels) with one blade tongue (0.25 mm modelled clearance); 3.4 mm hole; tensile XY / Z wet 7 days; one blade printed in the stated orientation | slip fit with no play; wet Z tensile >= 16 MPa (root bending 1.9 MPa at 2 m/s) | adjust XY compensation; thicker root; change the print orientation |
| G4 | Static seal (caps retained by the shell clamp) | nacelle assembled IN the shell clamp, vacuum -50 kPa 15 min; 24 h at 1 m; thermal cycle 35 C -> 5 C | decay < 1 kPa / 15 min; card dry | find the leak with +20 kPa and soap (clamp fitted: the lips hold the caps); re-seat the penetrator |
| G5 | Rotating seal and shaft tilt | 1 h at 400 rpm in a bucket at 0.3 m, then 8 h at 300 rpm; open and inspect; dial indicator on the rotor tip with a 10 N side load | dry inside; no scoring; tip motion <= 0.48 mm (single-bearing model, radial clearance 0.012 mm) | replace the seal; polish; if the tip motion exceeds the limit, fit the second bearing (698 in a longer boss) |
| G6 | Static (starting) torque of the rotor | rotor on the bench in a flume or towed at 0.5 / 0.6 / 0.7 m/s with the shaft locked: torque by thread + scale at 3 stop angles | >= 20 / 29 / 40 mN m (model 29 / 42 / 57 with the BEM-at-TSR-0.1 model; upper bound 51 at 0.5 m/s; breakaway to beat 36 mN m) | the starting-torque model is the least certain part of the BEM: re-fit `static_torque_bem_low_lambda` and re-run the selection |
| G7 | Self-start | in the stream at 0.5, 0.6, 0.7, 0.8 m/s (measured 0.3 m ahead of the rotor), converter in MPPT, from rest at 3 stop angles | starts within 60 s at the model's minimum (0.6 m/s baseline; bounds 0.4-0.8) | report the measured minimum; the device is still usable hand-started (sustain from 0.5 m/s) |
| G8 | Power curve | rpm, bus V/I, battery current at 0.5 / 0.6 / 0.7 / 0.8 / 1.0 m/s; log 10 min each; the 0.5 m/s point hand-started if needed | within the one-at-a-time envelope (-39 % / +14 % at 0.7 m/s) of 0.16 / 0.49 / 0.96 / 1.60 / 3.45 W net | update the polar knockdown (fig. 8), the friction and the inflow factor; revisit the chord scale |
| G9 | Converter behaviour | minimum input voltage at which it draws power; efficiency at 0.5 / 1 / 3 W input; quiescent + fixed overhead; MPPT hunting around the rotor's torque peak; battery current limit | V_min <= 5 V; eta >= 80 % at 1 W; fixed overhead <= 0.15 W (0.30 W costs 100 % at 0.5 m/s); never more than 1.35 A from the generator | choose the other converter candidate; add a bulk capacitor; add the current limiter |
| G10 | Survivability 1.0-2.0 m/s | at the fastest available flow: open-circuit bus (must stay <= 60 V TVS standoff: model 46 V at 2 m/s), bus current with the converter clamped (<= 1.35 A; the boost+clamp unit exceeds it from 1.8 m/s), blade tip clearance to the ring under load (static gap 5.4 mm; ring compliance 0.14 mm/N), no vibration; inspect the stake socket | as stated | current limiter / relay dump; stiffer ring (ribs); two-point mounting |
| G11 | Shallow-water and inflow effects | run at 0.6-0.8 m/s with the axis at 0.35 / 0.45 / 0.6 m depth and with the ring 50 mm above the bed; note air entrainment and surface dimpling; repeat with a temporary foam fairing over the front-cap step (inflow factor check: 0.70 costs 2 % at 0.7 m/s in the model) | output within 20 % of the deep case at >= 0.45 m; minimum deployment depth 0.35 m (socket + stake + ring + freeboard) | restrict the minimum deployment depth in the instructions; add the fairing to the shell clamp |
| G12 | Endurance | 50 h in the stream; inspect the seal, M5 joint (still tight), pins, bolts, blade roots, ring joints, socket, silt in the ring; moisture card | dry; no loosening; no cracks | redesign the flagged feature |

Minimum data set to close the model: G1 (generator), G2 (friction), G6 (starting torque), G8 (power curve). These four replace the
largest assumptions in order of their effect on the 0.5-0.7 m/s prediction.
