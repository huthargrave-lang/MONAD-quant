# Validation plan with acceptance criteria (redesign v3.1)

Analytical checks in this package are NOT tests. Nothing below has been performed. Each gate names the measurement, the
pass criterion and the action on failure. Instruments: optical tachometer (through the duct), DC V/I logger (10 Hz), thermocouples
or the winding-resistance method, a vacuum pump with gauge, a spring scale and thread for torque, a float or probe for water speed,
a dial indicator, a 250 V insulation tester.

| # | Gate | Measurement | Acceptance | On failure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Generator characterisation (bench, before machining) | KV (3 speeds, 3 line pairs), R_LL, L_LL, cogging torque (full turn), no-load loss at 700 rpm, bell runout, hole patterns (which is stator / bell), lead exit position, through-bore, mass | KV 20-26 rpm/V; R_LL 4.5-6.5 ohm; cogging <= 30 mN m; no-load loss <= 1.5 W at 700 rpm; runout <= 0.05 mm | update `system_model_v3.py` ASSUMPTIONS and the CAD placeholders; if Kt/sqrt(R) < 0.17 reject the motor |
| 2 | Printed coupons | tube-fit ring, airfoil pocket/stub, 3.0 mm horizontal holes with spring pins; tensile bars XY and Z and a short-beam shear bar, wet-conditioned 7 days | slip fit on the tube; tongue 0.1-0.3 clearance; spring pins hold 100 N pull; wet Z tensile >= 18 MPa, XY >= 32 MPa, interlaminar shear >= 11 MPa | change XY compensation / material; re-run `structure_seal_thermal_v3.py` with measured allowables |
| 3 | Machined parts | plug OD vs the bored tube lands; groove depth; bearing bores N7; seal seat; journals after anodising; sleeve width and fit; circlip lug envelope in the 24 mm recess; split-ring fit in its groove | gland squeeze 15-25 % computed from the measured pair; journals 15 h6; runout < 0.03; ring halves flush within 0.05 | rework / re-anodise |
| 4 | Static seal and breathing | vacuum -70 kPa (292 N per cap), hold 15 min; 24 h submersion at 1 m; thermal cycle: close the drum at 35 C, immerse at 5 C x3 and run 1 h at 1.2 A equivalent heating (load bank on the stator) at 0.3 m depth; moisture card inside | pressure decay < 1 kPa / 15 min; card dry after the cycles (tests the lip-lift / pumping case, R32) | find the leak with +30 kPa and soap; check the lip orientation, the O-ring, the plug; if the lip pumps, raise the minimum depth or fit a double-lip seal |
| 5 | Breakaway and running friction | thread-and-scale on the carrier, in air and submerged, cold (5 C) and after 24 h in silty water; all shaft angles; with the PTFE seal (baseline) and, if available, the NBR alternative | breakaway <= 0.047 N m (model, PTFE); running <= 0.016 N m at rest | NBR excluded; lower-cogging motor; check the sleeve finish and the labyrinth clearance |
| 6 | Rotating seal | 650 rpm 1 h and 1300 rpm 10 min at 0.5 m; then 8 h at 650 rpm at 2 m; vacuum test and open | dry; no water line inboard of the seal; no sleeve scoring | seal backwards or journal scored: replace; fallback bare anodised journal |
| 7 | Thermal | 2 h at 1.2 A generator current (load bank) with the drum in 20 C water; winding resistance before/after; boss and cap temperatures | winding rise <= 45 K (model +28 K metal path); boss <= 35 C | lower the Victron current limit; improve the stator-flange joint (paste, flatness) |
| 8 | Drum balance, overspeed and pins | static balance; spin to 1300 rpm in air (drill) and 1300 rpm in water; inspect the pins and the blade roots | no visible vibration; no blade or pin movement; runout unchanged; no root cracks | rebalance; check pins; thicker ligament |
| 9 | Self-start | river or flume at 0.9, 1.0, 1.2 m/s, controller in RUN, generator open: start from rest at every stop angle? | starts within 60 s at >= 1.0 m/s (target; the model says marginal x1.25 with PTFE, fails with NBR); report the measured minimum | accept push-start; lower-cogging motor; larger root chord |
| 10 | Power curve | rpm and DC V/I vs water speed at 1.0 / 1.5 / 2.0 m/s with the real duct; battery current; compare with the model's charging points | within -30 % / +10 % of REPORT table 7 (2.2 / 10.2 / 25.2 W DC) | update the model; revisit hub ratio or blade design |
| 11 | Protection and control | full battery (charger stops) at 2.0 m/s: bus rises, dump engages <= 45 V, settles at ~23 V and does not cycle for 10 min; flow lowered to 1.2 m/s: dump releases below 12 V and does not re-engage; disconnected load at 2.2 m/s: open-circuit bus <= 60 V; brake switch at <= 2.2 m/s stops the rotor within 10 s and holds 1 h at 2.0 m/s (winding rise <= 10 K); fuse opens on a shorted output; dump block temperature | as stated; no part in the dry box above 60 C; dump block case <= 40 C in water | re-tune the relay thresholds; resistor value; larger dump plate |
| 12 | Cavitation and overspeed observation | at the highest available flow (>= 2 m/s): run open-circuit for 1 min and with the dump engaged; listen / look for tip cavitation (hiss, bubbles); inspect the tips after 10 h at 2 m/s open-circuit | no erosion; if cavitation is audible open-circuit above 2 m/s, the dump must be the normal full-battery state (it is) | lower the open-circuit speed (lower KV / more load) or restrict the flow range |
| 13 | Endurance | 100 h in representative water; 2-week inspections (vacuum test, card, seal track, pins, bolts, RTV, blade roots and tips, labyrinth silt); seal change rehearsal at the first inspection (plan section 4) | dry; no loosening; no cracks at blade roots, pins or lips; seal change under 1 h without cutting anything but the RTV | redesign the flagged feature |

Service intervals (prototype): 2 weeks for the first 2 months; lip seal at 6 months or first moisture; carrier bolts re-torqued after
the first 24 h in the water (creep of the printed ears). Operating limits from the model: axis depth >= 0.3 m (seal pumping) and
provisionally <= 2 m (pressure case); 3 m/s is a survival case only (dump engaged; brake <= 10 min).
