# Correlation Idea Atlas: 100 model-factory seeds

**Research node:** `H253204`
**Date:** 2026-08-03
**Status:** deliberately unranked; no idea rejected for seeming far-fetched
**Purpose:** create broad parents that can later spawn source audits, event ledgers,
baselines, and models without collapsing back into one mean-reversion family

## How to read this atlas

Every row is a hypothesis seed, not an edge claim. “Expected relation” records a
direction worth attempting to falsify; a zero or opposite result is useful. Horizons are
initial measurement windows, not optimized parameters. The final column names the first
way an attractive correlation could be fake.

Ideas remain intentionally unranked. Later triage should score point-in-time source
quality, sample size, event purity, exposure mapping, transaction costs, redundancy,
redistribution rights, and performance under issuer-grouped forward validation.
Novelty or strangeness is not a rejection criterion.

## Public source palette

The atlas favors sources with machine-readable public interfaces:

- [SEC EDGAR APIs](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces)
  expose real-time submissions and standardized XBRL facts; issuer extensions and
  filing-calendar differences still require care.
- [FRED and ALFRED](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/docs/api/fred/overview.html) expose
  macro series and historical vintages, which are essential for preventing revision
  leakage.
- [Treasury FiscalData](https://fiscaldata.treasury.gov/datasets/treasury-securities-auctions-data/)
  supplies official auction fields, but not the pre-result when-issued quote needed for
  a true tail.
- [CFTC Commitments of Traders](https://www.cftc.gov/MarketReports/CommitmentsofTraders/index.htm)
  provides downloadable positioning history; Tuesday observation and Friday release
  clocks must remain separate.
- [EIA Open Data](https://www.eia.gov/opendata/index.php/api) includes hourly balancing-
  authority electricity operations plus fuel, storage, and price series.
- [NOAA Climate Data Online](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/cdo-web/webservices/v2) provides
  station and location weather data; forecast-time studies need forecast archives, not
  only realized observations.
- [BLS Public Data API](https://www.bls.gov/developers/home.htm) provides labor and price
  data; vintage handling remains necessary for revisions.
- [USAspending's public API](https://github.com/fedspendingtransparency/usaspending-api)
  supports award and transaction research, with award/obligation/modification clocks.
- [USPTO Open Data](https://data.uspto.gov/apis/bulk-data/search) exposes patent and
  trademark products, subject to authentication and assignment/entity work.
- [openFDA](https://open.fda.gov/apis/drug/enforcement/) exposes drugs, devices,
  enforcement, adverse-event, and shortage datasets with explicit source limitations.

## Narrowing later without killing novelty

When narrowing is authorized, first cluster ideas that share the same expensive
substrate. One source contract can serve multiple children:

1. **SEC filing factory:** C021–C040, C045–C050, and financing exposure for C001.
2. **Scheduled macro factory:** C001–C020 and C061–C070.
3. **Market microstructure factory:** C041–C060.
4. **Government-event factory:** C071–C080 and C099.
5. **Entity/geospatial factory:** C013, C062, C065–C067, C078–C079, C089, C094–C095.
6. **Attention/network factory:** C081–C090, C098, and C100.

The first narrowing pass should select infrastructure parents, not apparent backtest
winners. A source contract that can cheaply spawn ten honest studies may be more
valuable than one exciting but irreproducible correlation.
