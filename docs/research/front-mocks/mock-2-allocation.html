What does a mix mean?

A portfolio is rarely one thing. It is weights that add to 100%. Change the mix and watch how the blend path compares to each sleeve alone.

EXAMPLE DATA  Blend math is real; paths are sample. This is not an allocation advice tool. There is no “optimal” mix here.

Terms, in place

What “60/40” means

Sixty percent in a broad stock index, forty percent in bonds. A description of weights — not a brand and not a promise. When stocks fall, bonds sometimes cushion — and sometimes (as in 2022) both fall together.

Worked example: $10,000 → $6,000 index + $4,000 bonds. Rebalance means selling the one that grew to restore 60/40.

What a cash sleeve is for

Short bills (example: SGOV) barely move in price. Their job is optionality: money you can deploy after a washout without selling something you did not want to sell.

If cash is 10% of $100k, you have $10k of dry powder. You gave up some growth for that option.

What a gold sleeve is for

Gold is not a company. People use a small weight hoping it zigzags when stocks do not. That hope is a hypothesis — MONAD tested it and the clean holdout did not confirm it.

See the honest result panel before treating gold as “the diversifier.”

What “blend” means on the chart

Each month, the blend return is the weighted average of sleeve returns. The thick line is that path, indexed to 100 at the start — same window as the thin sleeve lines.

If you push one slider up, others shrink so the sum stays closed at 100%.

Honest negative result · gold sleeve

MONAD study language (paraphrased for this mock): a ~10% gold sleeve looked helpful on a recent window, but it was the best of three candidates and failed a clean out-of-sample holdout (2004–2013). Risk-adjusted confidence intervals straddled zero. Gold did not confirm.

That is why this page lets you add gold and watch the line — and why it will not call gold a “must-have diversifier.” Showing what did not work is the point.

ILLUSTRATIVE summary of a research finding · not investment advice

Worked example · reading the chart

  1. Set 60 / 40 (index / bonds). The blend sits between the two thin lines when they diverge.
  2. Move 10 points from bonds into gold. Ask: did the thick line become calmer — or just different?
  3. A prettier sample path is not evidence. The OOS panel is the evidence language this desk uses.
