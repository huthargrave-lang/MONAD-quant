What is this idea — and what would break it?

Twenty geopolitical / commodity buckets. Each is a story about a shock, a holding-period role, a short list of liquid study objects — and a fails line as important as the claim.

EXAMPLE DATA  Theses and fails lines are authored research objects. Heat is editorial opinion, not a measurement. Opening a card teaches the idea. Filtering comes after, and still never ranks a company.

Holding-period roles · why you would hold this

Insurance

Held because a bad event may pay the sleeve — not because you want the event. Cost of carry is expected.

Restock

Held for multi-month / multi-year backlog after inventories burn. Noisy on day one; thesis is the refill.

Order

Held for the flow of new contracts / capacity once policy or war spending shows up in orders.

Scare

Moves on headlines and risk premia; can reverse fast when the scare fades. Mechanism ≠ durable earnings.

Heat (0–4) is a researcher’s present judgment of how tightly this bucket couples to a named shock family — not a calculated score, not a forecast, not a rating. The bar is a length encoding of that opinion; the word “editorial” travels with it.

Those buckets are the last lesson. The screener is the desk: briefing, table, then apparatus — same objects, more instruments.
