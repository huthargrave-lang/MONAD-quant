What MONAD is doing today

Two jobs: value screening across ~225 US-listed names with named lenses, and geopolitical theses grouped into authored buckets.

  • A lens answers: “which rows match this fundamental pattern?”
  • A bucket answers: “what is the idea, and what would falsify it?”
  • A scenario answers: “if this shock path plays out, how does it transmit?”

What it will not do

  • It will not tell you what to buy.
  • It will not collapse a company into a single “score” traffic light.
  • It will not pretend a missing number is zero.
  • Editorial heat stays labeled as opinion.

What these nine columns are
P/E
Price divided by a year of earnings. Roughly, years of current profit to pay for the share. A fund has none, because a fund is not a company.
Div yield
Cash paid out per year as a share of price. A high one can mean a generous payer or a falling price. It does not say which.
On-BS D/E %
Debt against equity, counting only what is on the balance sheet. Leases, SPVs and supplier financing sit outside it, which is what the next column exists to catch.
AI shadow
Judged, not measured. A researcher's note on how a company is exposed to the AI buildout through obligations the D/E number misses. An opinion about structure, not a figure from a filing.
Beta
How much the share has moved when the market moved. Near 1 means it tracked; below 1 means it moved less. Backward-looking, and it changes.
Tone
Judged, not measured. How coverage of the name has read recently. It describes what was written, not what the business did.
Ticker, Name, Sector
Identity. The sector label is the vendor's, and vendors disagree at the edges.
