**Purpose:** answer the blunt question — *if everything goes to shit, what actually goes up?*

> **One-line thesis.** Most “safe havens” fail on day one because everyone sells liquid
> stuff to raise cash. What works is usually a **war hedge**: an asset whose *payoff
> rises because of the shock itself* (oil cut off, missiles spent, ships rerouted),
> not because it is “safe.”

## 0. The hard lesson from 2026 (Iran / Hormuz)

Empirical pattern after the late-Feb 2026 US–Israel–Iran escalation (academic + market writeups):

| Asset people expected to rip | What often happened day 0–4 | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Gold | Flat-to-down (~−4% in first days in some windows) | Crowded; sold for liquidity / margin |
| Defense stocks | Often sold with the tape first | Same de-risking; “already priced” |
| Bitcoin | No reliable haven | Risk asset in a risk-off tape |
| **Oil / gas** | Clearest short-run hedge | Shock *is* the supply cut |
| Tanker / war-risk freight | Rates & premiums explode | Insurance becomes the blockade |

**Ledger rule #1 — Safe haven ≠ war hedge**
- **Safe haven:** tries to *decouple* from risk assets (cash, short T-bills; gold over multi-week windows sometimes).
- **War hedge:** *pays because the war broke something* (Hormuz oil, munitions restock, Cape reroutes).

**Ledger rule #2 — Buy the mechanism, not the headline**
Ask: *What physical bottleneck just tightened?* Then own the thing that clears that bottleneck.

**Ledger rule #3 — Day-one dip is often the entry in crowded havens**
Gold/defense can dump on liquidity even when the thesis is right. Chaos Buckets separate **instant war hedges** from **structural fear assets**.

## 1. The twelve Chaos Buckets

Each bucket has:
- **Shock that lights it**
- **Mechanism** (why price rises)
- **Instruments to research** (ETFs / liquid names / speculative satellites)
- **Fails when…**
- **Speed** (hours / days / months)

### Bucket 01 — Liquid Fear (the only true day-one haven)
**Shock:** Everything — bank stress, war, margin spiral.  
**Mechanism:** Forced selling needs cash; USD T-bills absorb it.  
**Research objects:** short-duration Treasuries (`BIL`, `SGOV`), USD cash, money-market.  
**Fails when:** you needed *return*, not dry powder; inflation spike without recession.  
**Speed:** hours.  
**Role in sleeve:** dry powder to *buy* war hedges after the liquidity washout.

### Bucket 02 — Hormuz / Oil Scarcity
**Shock:** Strait of Hormuz disruption, Gulf infrastructure hit, war-risk spiral.  
**Mechanism:** ~1/5–1/3 of seaborne crude; insurance premiums act as soft blockade even without full closure.  
**Research objects:**
- Liquid: energy beta (`XLE`), integrateds (`XOM`, `CVX`)
- US non-Gulf supply: shale (`FANG`, `EOG`, `DVN` — research)
- Crude proxies: `USO` / futures (roll cost caution)
**Fails when:** spare capacity + SPR + demand destruction crush the spike; conflict short.  
**Speed:** hours–days.  
**2026 note:** GSAM-type analysis framed prolonged Hormuz closure as the non-linear tail ($100+ oil).

### Bucket 03 — Gas / LNG Replacement
**Shock:** Qatar/Gulf LNG offline; Europe/Asia scramble.  
**Mechanism:** US is structural LNG exporter; longer voyages + higher TTF/JKM.  
**Research objects:** `LNG` (Cheniere), other US LNG exporters / midstream; European gas-sensitive utilities (careful — can be *hurt* by price).  
**Fails when:** Qatar flows normalize fast; warm winter; storage full.  
**Speed:** days–weeks.

### Bucket 04 — The Insurance Weapon (tankers & freight)
**Shock:** Red Sea, Hormuz, Bab al-Mandeb, Taiwan approaches.  
**Mechanism:** War-risk premiums jump (2026 reports: multiples of hull value; Hormuz quotes from ~0.25% toward several %); ships reroute Cape → ton-miles ↑ → tanker rates ↑.  
**Research objects:** crude tanker names (`FRO`, `DHT`, `STNG`, `EURN` — research liquidity); product tankers; dry bulk if grain/ore reroutes.  
**Fails when:** ceasefire + premium ratchet slowly down; owners refuse to sail (no volume).  
**Speed:** days.  
**Edge:** markets underprice “insurance closed the strait” vs “navy closed the strait.”

### Bucket 05 — Steel Rain (munitions & missile defense)
**Shock:** High-intensity conflict burning PGMs, interceptors, artillery.  
**Mechanism:** Stockpiles deplete in weeks; restock takes years; budgets follow.  
**Research objects:**
- Primes: `LMT`, `RTX`, `NOC`, `GD`
- ETFs: `ITA`, `PPA`, `XAR`
- Spec satellites: munitions/specialty suppliers, solid rocket / energetics juniors (research)
**Fails when:** budget freeze, program cancel, peace dividend narrative; day-one liquidity wash.  
**Speed:** week-one noisy; **months–years** for backlog earnings.  
**2026 note:** NATO 5%-of-GDP path + US missile-defense budget step-ups = structural, not only headline.

### Bucket 06 — Cheap Mass (drones, counter-UAS, autonomy)
**Shock:** Attritional wars prove $30k drones beat $2M missiles sometimes.  
**Mechanism:** Procurement pivots to attritable mass + counter-drone.  
**Research objects:** `AVAV`, `KTOS`, `RKLB` (space launch adjacency), smaller counter-UAS private→public pipeline.  
**Fails when:** program delays, Pentagon accounting, valuation already in orbit.  
**Speed:** months (contract awards), years (production).  
**Pair with Book I:** these systems eat magnets, Sb, W, Ge — links to Sovereign Ledger Sails.

### Bucket 07 — Invisible War (cyber, EW, ISR, space)
**Shock:** Taiwan contingency, great-power gray zone, infrastructure sabotage.  
**Mechanism:** Software/sensor layer gets funded even when shipyards can’t expand fast.  
**Research objects:** cyber (`CRWD`, `PANW`, `ZS` — commercial; defense IT primes); space/ISR (`RKLB`, `NOC` space, `LMT` space); spectral defense-tech baskets.  
**Fails when:** multiple compression; “cyber” becomes a meme multiple with no DoD tie.  
**Speed:** weeks on headlines; years on programs.

### Bucket 08 — Hard Money After the Washout (gold / silver / miners)
**Shock:** Sustained conflict + fiscal blowout + central-bank diversification.  
**Mechanism:** Not day-one magic — **multi-week / structural** bid from CB buying, real-rate fear, debasement.  
**Research objects:** bullion (`GLD`, `IAU`), silver (`SLV`), miners (`GDX`, `GDXJ`), royalty (`WPM`, `FNV`).  
**Fails when:** real rates rip higher; dollar squeeze; crowded long gets liquidated again.  
**Speed:** unreliable day 0; stronger on **persistence**.  
**Playbook:** Bucket 01 cash → buy Bucket 08 *after* the liquidity dump if the conflict elongates.

### Bucket 09 — Atoms Not Molecules (uranium / fuel cycle)
**Shock:** Fossil chokepoint + AI power + Russia enrichment risk.  
**Mechanism:** Energy security → nuclear restart; HALEU/LEU scarcity.  
**Research objects:** `CCJ`, `URA`, `URNM`; fuel cycle (`LEU` — already Book I Keel); miners (`UEC`, `NXE`, `DNN` — research stage).  
**Fails when:** uranium spot overshoots then inventories flush; reactor delays.  
**Speed:** weeks (sentiment) to years (contract books).  
**Overlap:** Book I Fuel Cell archetype.

### Bucket 10 — Empty Granaries (fertilizer / ag / food)
**Shock:** Gas spike (ammonia), Black Sea grain, Red Sea food-container disruption.  
**Mechanism:** Fertilizer is frozen gas; food inflation is a political crisis.  
**Research objects:** `CF`, `NTR`, `MOS`; ag equipment selectively; some softs via futures (roll/contango care).  
**Fails when:** gas normalizes; big harvest; export bans reverse.  
**Speed:** days–months.  
**2026 note:** Hormuz/gas commentary explicitly tied fertilizer cost upside risk.

### Bucket 11 — Wartime Elements (critical minerals under fire)
**Shock:** China export licenses, Taiwan disruption to refining logistics, stockpile panic.  
**Mechanism:** Same as Book I — but **crisis tempo**. Antimony, tungsten, REE magnets, gallium/germanium.  
**Research objects:** Book I list — `UAMY`, `USAR`, `MP`, `UUUU`, `AREC`, `NB`, `ASX:LRV`.  
**Fails when:** China dumps to kill Western projects *and* Western floors disappear.  
**Speed:** days on export bans; years on mine build.  
**This is the bridge between Book I and Book II.**

### Bucket 12 — Silicon Siege (Taiwan / advanced semis)
**Shock:** Taiwan Strait crisis, blockade, insurance-driven shipping halt (~$2T+ trade).  
**Mechanism:** TSMC geography risk → scramble for US/allied capacity + equipment + materials.  
**Research objects:**
- “Away from Taiwan” capacity: `TSM` still complex (is the hedge or the risk?); US fab beneficiaries (`INTC` foundry path — contested), equipment (`ASML`, `AMAT`, `LRCX`, `KLAC`)
- Substrate / specialty materials / HBM memory chain
- Harsh truth: many semis *fall* on Taiwan risk because they *are* the exposure
**Fails when:** you bought the victim thinking it was the hedge.  
**Speed:** hours (risk-off) vs years (reshoring).  
**Advanced rule:** for Taiwan shock, prefer **equipment + materials + US power/grid** over fabless designers with Taiwan wafer risk.

## 6. Operating rules for Chaos Buckets

1. **Pre-write the trigger.** “If Hormuz war-risk > X% of hull for Y days → add Bucket 02/04.” Don’t invent thesis mid-panic.
2. **Separate calendars.** Day-one = Bucket 01 + maybe 02. Week-two = 05/08. Year-two = 09/11/Book I.
3. **Respect convexity vs beta.** Tankers and juniors are convex; `XLE` and `ITA` are beta. Don’t confuse them.
4. **Crowding check.** If gold is +30% vs 200dma into a war (2026 pattern), expect a liquidity dip *before* the haven bid.
5. **Never average into a victim.** Taiwan-risk fabless on a “semis always win” story is how Chaos Buckets fail.
6. **Link to Book I.** Persistent geopolitics → industrial policy → Sovereign Ledger names. Chaos is the spark; sovereignty is the hold.
